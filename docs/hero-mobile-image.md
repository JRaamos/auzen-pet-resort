Preserve their recognizable faces, proportions, markings and natural anatomy, not generic replacement breeds. Constraints: only these two dogs, no people or human limbs, no sunglasses, no toys, no extra animals, no collars with new logos, no text, no watermark, no UI frame, no embedded gradient overlay. Believable high-end pet editorial photography, not illustration, not plastic CGI, no heavy HDR.
